# Orbit Systems - IT and Security Policy

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be changed every 180 days.
Reusing any of your previous 5 passwords is blocked by the system.
All employees must use the company password manager, 1Password.

## Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every company account.
SMS codes are not accepted as a second factor. Use the authenticator app or a
hardware key.

## Devices
All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. The IT team verifies this
during onboarding. Company data must not be stored on personal devices.
Laptops must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Reporting incidents
Any suspected security incident - a lost laptop, a phishing email that was
clicked, or an unexpected login alert - must be reported to
security@orbit.example within 2 hours of discovery.

## Phishing drills
The security team runs a simulated phishing campaign once per quarter.
Employees who click twice in a 12-month period are enrolled in a 45-minute
refresher training session.

## Software
Installing software from outside the approved catalogue requires a ticket to
IT. Use of unapproved AI tools with customer data is prohibited.

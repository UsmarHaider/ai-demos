# Orbit Systems - Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave is accrued monthly at 1.83 days per month.

## Carry over
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the next year.
Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Days above the 5-day cap are forfeited
and are not paid out.

## Notice period for leave
Annual leave must be requested at least 14 days in advance.
Leave of more than 10 consecutive days must be requested 30 days in advance.

## Casual and sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of casual leave and 8 days of paid sick leave per year.
Sick leave of more than 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
Casual leave cannot be carried over.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## How to apply
All leave is requested in the Orbit People portal and approved by your direct
manager. If your manager does not respond within 3 working days, the request
escalates automatically to Ayesha Khan in People Operations.

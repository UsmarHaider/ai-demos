# Orbit Systems - Travel and Expense Policy

## Per diem
Domestic travel inside Pakistan is paid at a per diem of PKR 4,500 per day.
International travel is paid at a per diem of USD 85 per day.
Per diem covers meals and incidentals. It does not cover hotels or flights.

## Receipts
Receipts must be submitted within 7 days of returning from a trip.
Claims submitted after 30 days are not reimbursed.
Expenses under PKR 1,000 do not require a receipt.

## Local transport
Taxi and ride-hailing journeys are reimbursed up to PKR 2,000 per day.
Personal car mileage is reimbursed at PKR 45 per kilometre.

## Approvals
Any single expense above PKR 25,000 requires written approval from a director
before the money is spent. Retrospective approval is not granted.

## Flights and hotels
Economy class is the standard for all flights under 6 hours.
Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.
Hotels are capped at PKR 18,000 per night domestically and USD 180 per night
internationally.

## Payment
Approved expenses are paid with the monthly payroll run on the 25th of each month.
Claims approved after the 20th roll into the following month.

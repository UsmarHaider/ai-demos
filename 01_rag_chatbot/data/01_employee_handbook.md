# Orbit Systems - Employee Handbook

## About
Orbit Systems is a 240-person software company with offices in Lahore and Dubai.
The company was founded in 2017 by Hana Siddiqui and Bilal Raza.

## Working hours
Core working hours are 09:30 to 18:00, Monday to Friday.
Employees may shift their start time by up to 90 minutes with manager approval.

## Hybrid working
Orbit Systems runs a hybrid model. Every employee must be in the office on
Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Monday and Friday are remote by default.
Fully remote arrangements require approval from a director and are reviewed
every six months.

## Probation and reviews
New joiners are on probation for the first 3 months.
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in March and in September.
Promotion decisions are only made in the September cycle.

## Equipment
Every employee is issued a laptop on day one. The standard machine is a
MacBook Air M4 with 24 GB of RAM. Engineers working on simulation workloads
may request a MacBook Pro instead, approved by their engineering manager.
